## Limitations and decision

The corpus contains 105 generated repeated-template documents plus 11 fictional
Northstar demo documents used only for development. Neither sources nor labels
have received independent review. Scenario IDs separate splits but do not
establish independent realistic question diversity. Enrich held-out scenarios
and freeze reviewed labels before claiming product validation. A sample of 30
tasks/category has broad uncertainty; 6–10 users cannot establish product-market
fit.

Decision: **pending evidence and human approval**, not GO. Record GO / ITERATE /
NO-GO only after charter gates, blinded review and actual costs/user results are
available. Name approver, evidence links, risks and next actions.
