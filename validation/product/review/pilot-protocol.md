# Exploratory user validation protocol

Status: not executed. Recruit 6–10 consenting Product, Engineering and Support
participants from at least two teams. Do not include identifying data in Git.
External sessions are blocked until the safety gate in the charter is signed off.

Each participant completes four equivalent task pairs: impact, conflict,
ownership, and current versus superseded decisions. Assign AB/BA order equally;
use different equivalent scenarios, not the same question twice. Randomize labels
and do not reveal which variant includes the ontology. Record assigned tasks,
including abandoned tasks, errors and timeouts.

Collect task start/end and verified-answer time, independently adjudicated task
success, original-source lookup time, confidence before verification, observed
usability failures, and willingness to use the system. Interview on indispensable
capabilities, acceptable curation effort and willingness to sponsor an operational
pilot. Measure governance labor per accepted fact and update separately.

Check desktop/mobile, keyboard navigation, visible focus, 200% zoom, screen-reader
labels and contrast against WCAG 2.2 AA. This document is not an accessibility
conformance claim.

Store consent and observations privately. Publish only aggregated, redacted results.
Report median paired task-time reduction and participant counts. This small sample
is exploratory, not evidence of product-market fit. Record whether two teams and
an accountable sponsor genuinely wish to continue. No recruitment or interviews
have occurred as part of this implementation.
